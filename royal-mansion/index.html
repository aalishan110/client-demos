About The Royal Mansion

Royalty, Elegance & Hospitality

The Royal Mansion is a premium banquet and event venue on Main Saidpur Road, Satellite Town, Rawalpindi, known as one of the best event planners in the twin cities.

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, our team looks after the details so your family can enjoy the day.

  • Elegant hall with custom stage and floral décor
  • Themed set-ups for weddings and birthdays
  • Roadside location, easy for guests to find
  • Dedicated booking support for your date
