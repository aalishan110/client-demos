THE HOUSE / OUR POINT OF VIEW

An occasion is personal.
Its setting should be, too.

House of Bethak is an event planning and bespoke décor studio in Islamabad. Weddings, intimate ceremonies, birthdays, private gatherings and corporate occasions — each calls for a different kind of space.

Our work brings décor concepts, spatial design and event planning together. Soft drapes, cane textures, candlelight or a little colour: the details become part of the gathering.
