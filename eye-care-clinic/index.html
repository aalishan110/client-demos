What we do

Complete eye examination and vision care

From a routine sight test to children's eye problems, every visit starts with a detailed examination and a clear explanation.

Comprehensive eye exam

A full check of your vision and eye health, with the findings explained in plain language.

Contact lens fitting

Assessment and fitting by a contact lens specialist, so lenses are comfortable and suit your eyes.

Children's eye care

Cycloplegic refraction for children and diagnosis and treatment of amblyopia (lazy eye).

Short & long sight

Testing and correction for short-sightedness and long-sightedness with an accurate prescription.

Eye strain & screen fatigue

Help for tired, aching eyes and headaches from long hours on phones and computers.

Tear quality & screening

Tests for tear quality and screening for common eye conditions, with counselling on next steps.
