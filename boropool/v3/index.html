    02 Inside every guide

    Eight questions, answered honestly.

    People deciding to work abroad are rarely short of opinions. What they lack is one reliable place that lays out the requirements, the order of steps and the real cost — before money changes hands.

    Open a guide →

    1. Where the demand is

      Which roles, in which regions, and why employers are hiring from abroad.

    2. What you earn, and what life costs

      Typical pay set against rent, tax and everyday spending — not headline salaries alone.

    3. Whether your qualification counts

      How degrees and diplomas from Bangladesh are assessed, and what happens if they fall short.

    4. Registration and licensing

      The professional body, the paperwork, and the sequence it must be done in.

    5. Language tests

    05 Our approach
    We focus on real processes, realistic expectations and long-term outcomes.

    Clarity

    Requirements, order of steps and timelines set out plainly, so you understand each stage before you commit to it.

    Transparency

    Every fee and third-party cost is explained up front. If a route is unlikely to work for you, we say so.

    Realistic expectations

    Recognition outcomes and processing times vary. We plan around ranges and risks, never promises.
