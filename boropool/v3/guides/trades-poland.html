Skilled trades in Poland

Poland hires skilled tradespeople through employer-sponsored work permits. The legitimacy of the employer, the permit type and the terms of the contract matter more than any agent’s promise.

ProfessionSkilled trades
DestinationPoland
LanguagesEnglish
Reading time9 min

  2. Understand the permit

1

Verify the employer

Confirm the company exists, is registered and has a record of hiring from abroad.

You’ll need

  • Company name and registration details
  • Contact details you can check independently
  • Evidence of previous overseas hires

Watch out

A polished brochure proves nothing. Verify with sources the agent does not control.

2

Understand the permit

Know which permit applies and who applies for it, you or the employer.

You’ll need

  • The permit type named in writing
  • The name of whoever files the application
  • An expected timeline from the employer

Watch out

If nobody can say who applies for the permit, the route is not real yet.

3

Read the contract

Pay, hours, accommodation and deductions should be written down and match what you were told.

You’ll need

  • A contract in a language you understand
  • Itemised pay and deductions
  • Accommodation terms

Watch out

Verbal promises that are not in the contract do not protect you.

4

Visa and arrival

Prepare documents, travel and the first weeks on the ground.

You’ll need

  • Valid passport and permit documents
  • Travel plan
  • Emergency contacts and first-month budget

Watch out

Keep copies of everything and never hand over your original passport to a third party.

Realistic expectations

If an agent asks for large payments before a verified offer exists, stop and speak to us first.
