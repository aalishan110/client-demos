Master’s study in Canada

A Canadian study permit begins with an admission letter from a designated institution, and a realistic budget for tuition and living costs.

ProfessionStudents
DestinationCanada
LanguagesEnglish
Reading time10 min

1. Shortlist programmes2. Admission3. Proof of funds4. Study permit
1

Shortlist programmes

Match programmes to your background, budget and the career you want afterwards.

You’ll need

  • Your transcripts and degree
  • A budget range
  • A shortlist of designated institutions

Watch out

Do not pay a deposit before you have confirmed the institution is designated.

2

Admission

Prepare academic documents, language evidence and a clear statement of purpose.

You’ll need

  • Transcripts and certificates
  • Language test results
  • Statement of purpose and references

Watch out

Generic statements are easy to spot. Write about your own plan.

3

Proof of funds

Show tuition and living costs in the form the authorities expect.

You’ll need

  • Bank statements with a clear history
  • Sponsor documents, if relevant
  • Tuition payment evidence

Watch out

Large unexplained deposits in your account attract questions.

4

Study permit

Submit a complete application and prepare for any interview or biometrics step.

You’ll need

  • Admission letter
  • Proof of funds
  • Passport and photographs
  • Biometrics appointment

Watch out

Processing times change. Apply as early as the rules allow.

Realistic expectations

Study-permit rules have tightened. We check current limits before you pay any deposit.
