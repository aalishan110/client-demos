Nurses in Australia

Nursing in Australia runs through registration with the national regulator before you can practise. Assessment of your qualification comes first, and English-language evidence follows.

ProfessionNurses
DestinationAustralia
LanguagesEnglish
Reading time12 min

1

Qualification assessment

Your education is assessed against Australian standards to see whether it is substantially equivalent.

You’ll need

  • Certificates and transcripts
  • Registration history
  • Evidence of recent practice

Watch out

Gaps in recent practice can complicate an assessment. Document them.

2

English evidence

Accepted test results at the required level, or evidence that meets the regulator’s criteria.

You’ll need

  • A booking for an accepted test
  • Practice for each test component
  • Valid results at the time of application

Watch out

Component scores matter, not just the overall score.

3

Registration

Apply to the regulator and complete any bridging requirements it sets.

You’ll need

  • Identity documents
  • Assessment outcome
  • Any required bridging evidence

Watch out

You cannot practise as a nurse until registration is complete.

4

Visa pathway

Choose the visa route that fits your registration status and employer.

You’ll need

  • Registration or assessment outcome
  • Employer sponsorship details where relevant
  • Health and character documents

Watch out

Visa rules change. Confirm the current route before paying application fees.

Realistic expectations

Requirements differ by state and change over time. The published guide carries the current official links.
