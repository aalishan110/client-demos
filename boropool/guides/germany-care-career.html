Request an assessment

Understand the questions to ask about qualifications, language preparation and professional recognition before choosing your next step.

This is a sample preparation guide. It is not an eligibility assessment or a complete explanation of a country’s immigration rules. Requirements vary by role and individual circumstances.
01

Choose the role you want to investigate

Start with a specific role, such as care assistant, elderly-care worker or registered nurse. Similar job titles can involve different duties, qualifications and recognition processes. Write down your current responsibilities and compare them with the role you want to pursue.

02

Organise your professional documents

Keep a clear record of your education, training and work history. Check that names, dates and job titles are consistent across documents. A well-organised profile makes it easier to identify what information is missing before you begin a formal process.

  • Education and training certificates
  • Employment records and references
  • A clear, up-to-date professional CV
  • Language learning or assessment records
03

Build a realistic language plan

Ask which language level applies to your intended role and pathway. Plan study time, course costs and any assessment preparation around your existing work commitments. A language course and professional recognition are separate parts of your preparation.

04

Understand the complete cost

Look beyond a single application fee. Request a written breakdown of services and find out which costs are paid to third parties. The table below shows the areas to discuss before committing to a pathway.

Cost areaWhat to clarify
Language preparationCourse fees, learning materials and assessment costs
Qualification recognitionAssessment, document preparation and translation fees
Application processOfficial fees, professional services and payment terms
RelocationTravel, accommodation and initial living expenses
05

Questions to ask before proceeding

Ask who the employer is, what the proposed role involves and how the application process works. Take time to review the written terms. Confirm the relevant requirements with official authorities and keep copies of all documents and receipts.

06

Prepare for your initial assessment

Send BOROPOOL a concise summary of your profession, country of residence, qualifications and years of experience. Mention your preferred destination and any language training you have completed. The first conversation can then focus on your actual background and the questions that need further investigation.
