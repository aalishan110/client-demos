# BOROPOOL sample website v1

Preliminary website concept by aaliSoft. Demonstration content and sample data.

This is a static design sample, including an English landing page and standalone English and Bengali career guides. The assessment form is a preview only and sends or stores no data. The floating WhatsApp control is disabled.

Bengali demonstration content was prepared with Google Translate and is subject to client review. BOROPOOL branding comes from its public LinkedIn profile. Photographs are illustrative Unsplash images, not BOROPOOL staff or clients.
