BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few useful
clarifications.

A clear first conversation starts with knowing what to expect.

Who is an initial assessment for?

Professionals considering an international career who would like to discuss their qualifications, work experience and intended direction with BOROPOOL.

What information should I prepare?

Your profession, country of residence, main qualifications and years of experience. Include any preferred destinations and language preparation. You do not need to upload a passport or sensitive documents in this sample.

Is this a job application or visa application?

No. The initial assessment request starts a conversation about your professional profile. It is separate from an employer’s recruitment process and any official application.

Does an assessment guarantee an outcome?

No. Employment, professional recognition and immigration decisions depend on the applicable requirements and the relevant organisations. Guidance should help you make informed decisions.

Can I read guides in Bengali?

Yes. This Bangladesh sample includes a mix of English and Bengali guides. Each article identifies its language, and the library can be filtered by language.
