A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Your questions,
answered.

Who is the career assessment for?

Professionals considering an international career who want to discuss their qualifications, work experience and goals with BOROPOOL.

Does an assessment guarantee a job or visa?

No. Opportunities depend on your profile, employer requirements and the applicable official processes. BOROPOOL’s guidance is intended to help you make informed decisions.

What should I prepare before getting in touch?

A summary of your profession, country of residence, qualifications and years of experience. You can also mention any destinations you are considering.

Can I read career guides in Bengali?

This Bangladesh concept includes Bengali sample guides with English website navigation. Final languages, country versions and content will be confirmed with BOROPOOL.
